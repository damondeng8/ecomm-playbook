# 00 · Product and offer

> **Goal:** one product that a reachable group already wants, with enough margin to pay for its own advertising, and an offer that is easy to say yes to.
> **Agent: read this when** starting Phase 0 or the human isn't sure what to sell. No code until the product passes the scorecard.

## What sells online

Common traits: the result shows in three seconds of video, it solves a problem people already spend on, it is tied to an identity (runner, new parent, dog owner), it is light to ship, and it runs out or has a next product.

## Scorecard

Score each 1–5. Build if the total is 25+ of 35 and nothing scores 1.

| # | Criterion | 5 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Existing desire** | People already buy worse versions | You'd have to explain why they need it |
| 2 | **Demoability** | Before and after in 3 seconds | Needs a paragraph |
| 3 | **Margin** | Landed cost under 25% of price | Over 50% |
| 4 | **Repeat or next purchase** | Refills monthly, or a clear second product | One and done |
| 5 | **Reachable audience** | A named group with its own creators and pages | "Everyone" |
| 6 | **Shipping** | Small, light, unbreakable | Bulky, fragile, regulated |
| 7 | **Low risk** | No health, safety, or legal exposure | Ingestible, medical, for children |

## Steps

1. List 5 groups you belong to or can reach.
2. For each, list what they already buy and complain about. Read 1–3★ reviews of the best sellers in the category. The complaints are your product brief and your copy ([15](15-copy.md)).
3. Search TikTok and Reels for the problem. Look for "where did you get this?" comments.
4. Write down the awareness level and the sophistication stage of the market ([15](15-copy.md)). A stage 3 market needs a new mechanism, not a louder claim.
5. Get the landed cost: product, packaging, freight, duty.
6. Do the math in [12](12-costs.md). Contribution margin on the first order must cover what it costs to get the customer, or the repeat rate must be proven.
7. Build the offer ([07](07-payments-pricing.md)): bundle, bonus, guarantee, shipping.
8. Validate in 1 week, before buying stock:
   - 10–15 short videos with a sample or a mockup.
   - A one-page presale or waitlist (a Worker + D1), with the real price on it.
   - Signal: saves, shares, "where can I buy this?", more than 10% of visitors leave an email, or real presale orders.
9. Write the one-liner: "For **[who]**, **[product]** gets **[result]** without **[the thing they hate]**."

## Domain guide

| Domain | Watch out for |
|---|---|
| **Supplements, food, drink** | Health claims, labeling rules, payment-processor restrictions, insurance |
| **Skincare and cosmetics** | Ingredient and claim rules, patch-test advice, before/after honesty |
| **Apparel** | Sizing returns, inventory spread across sizes |
| **Kids and baby** | Safety testing and certification |
| **Electronics** | Certification, batteries in shipping, warranty returns |
| **Pets** | Ingestible rules, "see a vet" for anything medical |
| **Home and fitness gear** | Shipping cost and damage |

## Avoid

- Products you can't show working.
- Anything a marketplace sells identically for less.
- A category where you can't make a true, specific claim.
- Launching with more than three products.
